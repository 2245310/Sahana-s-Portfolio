# Sahana Ramesh | Portfolio

A collection of selected works, experiments and explorations in visual communication and strategic branding.

I'm Sahana, a visual communication and strategic branding student at Srishti Manipal Institute, Bangalore. I enjoy turning observations, ideas and curiosities into meaningful visual experiences.

My practice focuses on:

- **Branding:** Building thoughtful brand identities through strategy and visual storytelling.
- **Publication:** Exploring ideas through editorial design, typography and visual narratives.
- **Packaging:** Creating purposeful visual experiences that connect products with people.

## About the Portfolio

This website brings together selected academic projects, personal explorations and creative experiments. It documents not just the final outcomes, but also the thinking, research and processes behind them.
